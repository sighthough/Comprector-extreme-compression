Mathematical Mechanics

Flag-based coding adds 1 bit per block. A match encodes to 1 + P bits; a miss expands to 1 + N bits.

E(L) = p · (1 + P) + (1 - p) · (1 + N)
pbreak-even = 1 / (N - P)
